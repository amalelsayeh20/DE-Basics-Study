Normalization

to eliminate ⇒ data redundancy
⇒ data anomalies

what it solve

1) insertion anomaly

2) update anomaly

3) deletion anomaly

• First Normal Form: 1NF

↪ every column must contain (indivisible values)
   ❄ separate repeating items into
     individual rows (one scalar value)

• Second Normal Form: 2NF

↪ every non-key attribute must fully
     depend on the entire Primary key.

• Third Normal Form: 3NF

↪ non-key columns can only depend
     on the Primary key.

• BCNF → resolve rare anomalies where a table
     has multiple overlapping composite
     candidate keys.
